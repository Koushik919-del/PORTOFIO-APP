My Hobbies

When I'm not coding or messing with servers, I enjoy spending my time doing a few other things:

I play a lot of story-driven and open-world games. God of War and Minecraft are some of my all-time favorites, which is why they end up inspiring my coding projects too.

Tinkering with Tech

I love taking things apart. Whether it's setting up a RetroPie, configuring a Pi-Hole, or just seeing how an old laptop works, hardware is a big hobby of mine.

Exploring AI

Seeing what AI can do and pushing it to its limits is super interesting to me. I like building tools that use AI to make life a bit easier.

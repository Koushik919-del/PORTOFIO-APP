Pi-Hole AdBlocker

A network-level ad blocker running 24/7 on my Raspberry Pi 3. It runs on the local network to keep my internet clean. It also is with TailScale, so wherever I go around the world, it will always work. It also has Unbound so I have privacy around the internet with a VPN, and there are no third parties.

Learn to make your own with this video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6sznCZ7ttbI

PlayPi-4

A custom RetroPie build on the Raspberry Pi 4, with emulators of Sega, Nintendo, PSP, PS1, and more. It has games with the ROMs I have. In the future, I want to turn it into a real console, where it is a handheld but if it connected to a device it streams it in there like a Nintendo Switch. The name came from combining the names Playstation and Raspberry Pi 4.

Watch this video to make your own: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AaseHnf0k2o
